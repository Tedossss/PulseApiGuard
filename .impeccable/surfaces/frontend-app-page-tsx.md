# Root portfolio — The Remembered Street

- Mode: Experience. Recruiters, hiring managers, and prospective clients should identify Nazar as a full-stack engineer, inspect six verified projects, and reach contact without losing the sense of discovery.
- Direction: one empty daylight Dreamcore neighborhood remembered incorrectly—huge blue sky, rolling green ground, pastel domestic buildings, civic signs, analog grain, mild haze, and small impossible events. No observatory, vivarium, sci-fi, generic project grid, or photographic scene layer.
- Approved composition: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/dreamcore-remembered-street.webp` (`Street-Sign Arrival`). Half quiet sky; physical identity sign lower-left; S-path from lower-right; PulseGuard near-right; five distinct landmarks recede toward a tiny water tower; six-stop directory remains fixed along the bottom.
- Journey: guided scroll through entrance, six project parcels, About, and Contact. HTML owns every name, fact, state, and action. WebGL owns the continuous neighborhood, camera travel, landmark behavior, and restrained ambient anomalies.
- Landmarks: PulseGuard is the awake watch-house with three failure lamps; CoLab is two houses joined by one impossible room; Foundation is a bilingual civic publishing house; Prime Leather is a split repair workshop stitched through its roof and facade; BookShelf is a home whose roof becomes a shelf; Local LLM is a prepared-but-not-run computer classroom.
- Memorable moment: each system becomes understandable as a place before its evidence panel opens; the active property wakes while the rest stay quietly visible along the same road.
- Controlled surrealism: a wrong-direction sign shadow, one floating door, identical repeated clouds, and one far sidewalk loop. These remain secondary, comforting, and strange rather than horrific.
- Constraints: consume verified project truth from `frontend/app/dreamworld/dreamData.ts`; retain six-project navigation, direct links, contact/CV actions, keyboard access, visible focus, mobile WebGL reduction, reduced-motion still frames, and a complete CSS fallback. Never composite a generated background image behind the canvas.
- Finish review: `PASS` after the reduced-motion loader, mobile arrival hierarchy, and procedural 3D finish blockers were rebuilt and re-tested at 1440×810 and 390×844.
